Account Registration & Security

To utilize the EduMatch matching engine and project collaboration tools, users must register for an authorized account:

  • You must be at least 16 years of age or enrolled in a recognized secondary or higher education institution.
  • You agree to provide true, accurate, current, and complete information during registration and keep your profile updated.
  • You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials, passwords, and API tokens.
  • Multiple accounts, identity spoofing, or creating accounts to circumvent previous suspensions is strictly prohibited.
Security Reminder: EduMatch will never solicit your password through unsolicited chat, email, or third-party messaging services. Notify support immediately if you suspect unauthorized account access.

Student Responsibilities & Project Requests

Students utilize EduMatch to discover qualified mentors, project guides, and developers. When creating requirements or placing orders:

  • Clear Scope Definition: Requirement posts must contain genuine project parameters, technology stacks, deadlines, and fair budget expectations.
  • Respectful Communication: All messaging across our real-time chat must remain courteous, professional, and free of harassment.
  • Prompt Milestone Reviews: When a provider delivers code, project documentation, or a milestone artifact, students must inspect and provide constructive feedback within 5 business days.
4

Provider & Mentor Professional Standards

Project Providers and Technical Mentors represent the foundation of quality across EduMatch. Providers agree to adhere to the following benchmarks:

  • Honest Skill Representation: Providers must only claim proficiency in technologies, languages, and frameworks they genuinely possess.
  • Deliverable Quality: Code deliverables must be runnable, clean, documented, and accompanied by execution instructions.
  • On-Time Delivery: In the event of an unavoidable delay, providers must communicate with the student at least 24 hours prior to the agreed milestone deadline.
  • No Malicious Code: Delivering spyware, ransomware, backdoors, or malicious scripts results in immediate permanent ban and legal escalation.

Academic Integrity & Ethical Guidelines

EduMatch was conceived as an educational platform to connect students with experienced software engineers, data scientists, and peer mentors to facilitate learning, technical guidance, and collaborative project development.

Important Ethical Notice: EduMatch promotes guided learning, architectural walkthroughs, debugging assistance, and prototype coaching. Students remain solely responsible for understanding the software they submit to their colleges or universities and adhering to their institution's specific honor code and academic integrity regulations.
  • Providers are encouraged to provide code comments, architectural diagrams, and video explanations to ensure the student understands the underlying concepts.
  • EduMatch explicitly forbids impersonation in live examinations, academic assessments, or prohibited unauthorized third-party exam completion.
6

Pricing, Settlement & Milestone Agreements

EduMatch facilitates transparent and fair financial collaboration between students and providers:

  • Agreed Budgets: Project pricing is established upon offer acceptance and cannot be arbitrarily inflated mid-development without scope modification.
  • Milestone Progression: Projects follow progressive statuses: Pending → Accepted → In Progress → Delivered → Completed.
  • Reminders & Settlement: Providers may initiate payment reminders upon full milestone fulfillment. Students agree to settle verified deliverables in a timely manner.

Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Ownership rights for work produced on the EduMatch platform are defined as follows:

  • Custom Project Code: Upon complete settlement of the agreed project fees, the student receives full, perpetual ownership and copyright to the custom code created specifically for their order.
  • Pre-Existing Tools & Libraries: Open-source packages, third-party libraries, and pre-existing provider frameworks remain subject to their original licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0, GNU).
  • EduMatch Platform Assets: The EduMatch brand, matching algorithms, user interface, and proprietary software remain the exclusive property of EduMatch.

Dispute Resolution & Platform Mediation

In the rare instance that a student and provider cannot reach mutual agreement regarding project specifications or deliverable completeness:

  • Either party may open a formal ticket via the Dispute Resolution Center within the portal.
  • EduMatch platform administrators will inspect the initial project requirements, chat logs, code commits, and milestone timestamps.
  • Both parties agree to participate in platform mediation in good faith before seeking external legal remedies.
